Questions

Why does Elev8U take a commission at all?

It's what funds vetting, dispute support, and Evolute's matching — the same reason every marketplace works this way.

What happens if I don't book anything on the Free plan?

Nothing. R0 means R0 — no charge in a slow month, ever.

Can I switch between Free and Pro anytime?

Yes. Monthly Pro is month to month with no lock-in. Yearly Pro is billed once and runs for 12 months.

Why do clients pay a fee if the platform is "free"?

Browsing, chatting with Evolute, and getting matched cost nothing. The fee only applies once you're actually booking — same as Fiverr or Upwork's buyer fee, just lower.
